# 7. AI / Editorial Contamination and Authenticity

## 7.1 Four Classifications
When analyzing an author's corpus, observed writing patterns are categorized into one of four classes:
1. **AUTHENTIC:** Strong empirical evidence across unassisted writing samples demonstrating that the characteristic genuinely belongs to the author.
2. **AMPLIFIED:** A trait that is authentically personal to the writer, but appears disproportionately intensified in samples with known AI or editorial editing.
3. **CONTEXTUAL:** A pattern that is valid in specific subgenres (e.g. executive summaries), but not representative as a universal trait across the author's work.
4. **AI-RISK:** A synthetic rhetorical artifact that reflects machine-shaped rhetorical optimization rather than human authorial intent.

## 7.2 Risk Patterns
The framework identifies and monitors 10 distinct AI-shaped contamination patterns:
- **Rhetorical symmetry:** Artificial balancing of parallel clauses or bulleted triplets.
- **Manufactured insight:** False epiphany framing (e.g. "The real question isn't X; it's Y").
- **Premature compression:** Rushing to aphorisms before laying foundational reasoning.
- **Generic transitions:** Overused formulaic connectors ("Furthermore", "In summary", "Crucially", "Delving deeper").
- **Repeated X-not-Y constructions:** Overusing contrastive negation for superficial gravitas.
- **Excessive conceptual labels:** Inventing pseudo-intellectual phrases for common ideas.
- **Uniform cadence:** Monotonous sentence length variation devoid of natural thought stops.
- **Mandatory punchlines:** Ending every single paragraph with a self-important concluding sentence.
- **Formulaic questions:** Inserting rhetorical questions merely to break up text without genuine investigative need.
- **Over-polished paragraph architecture:** Clinical, textbook paragraph structures that strip idiosyncratic authorial asymmetry.

## 7.3 Important Rule
**AI-like does not automatically mean inauthentic.**
A crisp sentence, an insightful question, a clean contrast, or an elegant transition may be genuinely characteristic of a skilled human writer. Classification requires cross-referencing against verified unassisted samples and contextual need.

## 7.4 Reference Differential
$$\text{Observed Corpus} = \text{Authentic Voice} + \text{AI/Editorial Overlay} + \text{Contextual Variation}$$

The objective of the humanizer engine is to extract and reconstruct the **authentic personal signal** while stripping away avoidable synthetic overlay.

## 7.5 Anti-Imitation Principle
**Imitate decision-making, not stylistic statistics.**
Do not reproduce surface artifacts blindly; emulate the underlying cognitive choices that led to those stylistic decisions.
