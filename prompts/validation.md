# Validation Engine Prompt

Compare source draft and humanized output.

Check:
1. **Meaning Fidelity:** Did any factual claim, premise, or argument alter?
2. **Reasoning Fidelity:** Does the deductive sequence hold?
3. **Evidence Discipline:** Did any claim become stronger or weaker than the evidence supports?
4. **Voice Fidelity:** Does the text reflect the user's authentic decision tendencies?
5. **Genre Fitness:** Does the candidate match target context expectations?
6. **AI Template Density:** Have synthetic transitions and manufactured symmetries been removed?
7. **Rhetorical Overfitting:** Has the author's style been turned into a clumsy caricature?
8. **Artificial Imperfection:** Were any errors or typos introduced? (Must be strictly 0).

Check against Failure Taxonomy (F01 through F18).
Report failures and recommend targeted revision rather than rewriting blindly.
