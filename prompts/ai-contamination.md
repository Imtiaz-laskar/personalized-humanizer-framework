# AI Contamination Analysis Prompt

Identify observed patterns that may have been amplified by AI or editorial assistance.

Do not call a pattern AI simply because it is polished or syntactically clean.
Distinguish genuine authorial clarity from artificial machine-shaped rhetorical tropes.

Examine:
- Rhetorical symmetry & artificial parallelisms
- Manufactured insight & false epiphanies ("The real question isn't X; it's Y")
- Premature compression & soundbite endings
- Generic AI transitions ("Furthermore", "In summary", "Crucially", "Delving deeper")
- Repeated X-not-Y antitheses
- Excessive conceptual branding
- Monotonous rhythmic cadence
- Mandatory paragraph punchlines

Classify traits as:
- `authentic`: verified by unassisted writing
- `amplified`: personal trait intensified by AI editing
- `contextual`: valid in select formats
- `ai-risk`: synthetic machine artifact

Recommend action: `preserve`, `contextualize`, `reduce`, or `suppress`.
