# Genre Analysis Prompt

Analyze how the writer's voice changes by genre, audience, and purpose.

Identify changes in:
- density (cognitive and informational)
- rhythm and clause structure
- evidence and citation expectations
- qualification and hedging intensity
- question frequency
- analogy and metaphor use
- contrast and antithesis
- register and vocabulary
- conclusion patterns

Preserve the underlying personal voice while modeling contextual expression.
Treat cognitive density and rhetorical compression as independent axes.
